## Reading
- **Forecast (5.4)** is the runaway top: highest reach & impact, speaks in week one, cheapest of the
  high-value ideas, pure arithmetic.
- **WhatsApp digest (3.6)** ties annual summary on score but wins on strategic weight — it's the only
  idea that directly attacks the #1 risk (the second adult). It's the spine everything else is
  delivered through.
- **Annual summary (3.6)** is the delight/effort steal — an afternoon on top of existing code.
- **Drift (3.15)** scores well but is *silent for ~9 months* by its own gate, so it's Now-to-build /
  Later-to-speak; pair it with the forecast so there's always something to say.
- **Live sync (2.1)** is dragged down by Effort (L), not value. It's the big rock — Next, scheduled.
- Recurring (2.4) is a solid, cheap insight engine that also feeds fixed-vs-free.
